In -teen numbers, stress the last syllable and hold its ii longer, matching the recording. In -ty numbers, stress the first syllable and say the final i short, with a full *t*.

This works the same way in both styles, and it helps avoid mistakes with prices, dates and quantities.

## The th in three

Three, thirteen and thirty all start with th. Put the tip of your tongue lightly between your teeth and blow air out. If your top teeth touch your lower lip instead, three sounds like free; if your tongue stops the air, it sounds like tree.
