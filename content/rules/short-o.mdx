## Two sounds for the letter u

Most words spelt with u use the vowel in hut (*hat*): but, cup, bus, much. A short list uses a short *u* instead: put, push, full, sugar, butcher. The same short *u* is spelt oo in look, book and took.

<LetterUMap />

You can't tell from the spelling: but and put look alike but sound different. Learn the short list of words below and say every other u as a short *a*, starting from your Kinyarwanda *a*. Say luck as *lak*, not *luk*, which sounds like look.
