Start with a clear *t* in water, better and twenty. The full *t* we practise here is close to the *t* you already use in Kinyarwanda. Listen to the example, then keep that clear tongue contact when you repeat the word.

Use a clear *t* in these practice words. This is a speaking exercise: spellings such as th and -tion follow other patterns, and some words have a silent *t*.

<Callout variant="check">
  A full <Nowrap>*t* <Hear id="snd-t" label="t" />:</Nowrap> press the tip of your tongue on the ridge behind your top teeth so the air stops completely, feel the
  pressure build, then release it. If your tongue only taps the ridge, the *t* can sound like d.
</Callout>

<TRuleFlow />

<Callout variant="why">
  A clear *t* gives you a familiar movement to practise and helps distinguish words such as writing and riding.
</Callout>
