Say every t you see in the spelling as a full t, as in Standard English. The only exceptions are the silent-t words listed below.

<Callout variant="check">
  A full t: press the tip of your tongue on the ridge behind your top teeth so the air stops completely, feel the
  pressure build, then release it. If your tongue only flicks the ridge and the air never stops, you have made the
  American tapped t.
</Callout>

<TRuleFlow />

<Callout variant="why">
  The American tapped t in water and better is almost exactly the Kinyarwanda r. If you copy it, better sounds like
  'berrer' to most listeners outside North America, or 'beller' if your r slides towards l.
</Callout>
