## Where American English differs

Practise the Standard English form shown here. Listen to the American example so you can recognise another pronunciation of the same word.

The 'Some listeners may hear' column highlights possible confusions to explore in practice. What a listener hears also depends on the context and the pronunciation they are used to.

Words such as tomato, schedule or the letter Z are said differently in American and Standard English, but listeners
